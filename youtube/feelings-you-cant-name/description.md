Every Feeling You Can't Name, Explained (You've Had All of These)

You've felt all of these — you just never had the words. Psychologists have mapped the human emotional world far beyond the "5 feelings" we learn as kids, and naming them literally changes how your brain reacts. Here's the science of the feelings you can't name… and why learning their names gives you back control.
