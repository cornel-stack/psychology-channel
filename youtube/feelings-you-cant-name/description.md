Every Feeling You Can't Name, Explained (You've Had All of These)

You've felt all of these — you just never had the words. This is a deep, evidence-based tour of the emotions psychology has mapped but most of us were never taught to name… and why learning their names measurably changes how you feel and act.

Every on-screen claim is a real, peer-reviewed paper — and we zoom into the exact sentence that backs it. Paywalled studies are shown as honest citation cards. Full sources and DOIs below.
